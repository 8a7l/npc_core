# NPC Core — Player Guide

A short guide for players who just want to interact with NPCs.

## Talking to an NPC

1. Find an NPC in the world (humanoid figure with a nametag).
2. **Right-click** on them to open a dialog.
3. Read the text, then click one of the reply options on the right side.
4. Click **Close** to end the conversation.

## Nametag icons

Some NPCs show icons before their name:

| Icon | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| `$`  | Has a shop — you can trade items |
| `→`  | Has teleports — you can travel |
| `!`  | Has a dialog — you can talk |

For example: `[$!] Arthur` — has a shop and a dialog.

## Trading

If an NPC has a `$` icon, they might show a **Show wares** option in dialog.

1. Click **Show wares** (or similar).
2. A shop window opens with a list of items on the left.
3. Click an item to see what you give and what you receive.
4. If you have the required items, click **Exchange** to complete the trade.
5. Your items are removed, new ones are added to your inventory.

Example trade:
- You give: `10 × Steel Ingot`
- You receive: `1 × Steel Sword`

## Teleporting

If an NPC has a `→` icon, they might have teleport options in dialog.

1. Click the destination (e.g. **To City**).
2. If the teleport is paid, you must have the required item.
   - Example cost: `1 × Mese Crystal`
3. If you have it, the item is removed and you are teleported.
4. If not, you'll see: **Not enough resources for teleport.**

## Resident directory

Some NPCs (like the Wise Man) can show a directory of all residents.

1. Click **Tell about residents** (or similar).
2. The directory opens — list on the right.
3. Click a name to see their info:
   - Name
   - Profession
   - Location
   - Short description

## Tips

- **Nametags are hidden far away.** If you don't see icons above an NPC,
  walk closer.
- **Each NPC is independent.** Two NPCs with the same name may have
  different trades or dialogs — always read before clicking.
- **Shops do not remember you.** If you leave and come back, your
  selected trade resets. Your inventory is not affected.

## Common issues

**Q: I right-click an NPC but nothing happens.**
A: The NPC might not have a dialog configured. Ask the server admin.

**Q: The teleport says "Not enough resources" but I have the item.**
A: Check the exact amount — some teleports require more than 1.

**Q: The shop won't let me trade.**
A: Check that you have the exact items and quantity in your inventory.
   The shop message tells you what is missing.
